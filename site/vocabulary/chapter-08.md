### 本章 Language Takeaway

优先尝试主动说出的三个表达：

- **paraphrase an expression**：改述一种表达。
- **interpret the meaning**：解释含义；meaning 为补充词。
- **communicate clearly**：清楚沟通。

### 串学示范｜自拟短文

Learning a language involves more than vocabulary. Pronunciation and intonation affect how an utterance sounds, while grammar and syntax help organise its meaning. A learner can paraphrase a complicated expression, discuss an idea and practise negotiating. These activities connect language knowledge with communication.

**中文理解：** 学习语言涉及的内容超过词汇。发音和语调影响话语听起来的样子，语法和句法帮助组织意义。学习者可以改述复杂表达、讨论想法并练习协商，把语言知识与沟通连接起来。
