### 本章 Language Takeaway

优先尝试主动说出的三个表达：

- **an annual review**：年度回顾；review 在第 5 章出现。
- **for a short duration**：持续较短时间；short 为补充词。
- **at regular intervals**：每隔固定时间；regular、interval 为补充词。

### 串学示范｜自拟短文

A clear account explains when events happened and how long they lasted. We can describe an initial stage, an intermediate stage and a final result. Meanwhile introduces something happening at the same time. Daily, monthly and annual describe frequency, while permanent describes a state expected to continue.

**中文理解：** 清楚的叙述会说明事情何时发生、持续多久。我们可以描述初始阶段、中间阶段和最终结果。meanwhile 引出同时发生的事情；daily、monthly、annual 表示频率，permanent 描述预期持续的状态。
