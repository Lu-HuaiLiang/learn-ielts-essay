# 保护所有动物还是部分动物

<div class="essay-meta">

N04 · [动物保护](/topics/animal) · [双边讨论](/types/discussion) · 381 words

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>

尽量保护更多物种，同时按具体风险优先分配关注和资源；不是先支持全部、最后又取消部分动物资格。

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>情态动词后的被动</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

they should distinguished from other species by the threat the specific animals face and the value of the animal itself

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

they should be distinguished from other species by the threats they face and their value

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

情态动词后的被动结构必须是 should be distinguished。后半句用 they/their 统一指代这些动物，并用 threats 表示可能存在多种威胁，避免 specific animals、animal itself 在单复数和指代上来回切换。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>紧迫性与生态价值</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the importance of bees is diminished by large population size and ubiquity

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

the urgency of protecting a particular bee species may be lower if it has a large and stable population

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

常见或数量多，并不意味着生态价值更低。修改保留原来的资源排序思路，但比较的是某种蜂类保护的“紧迫性”，而非蜜蜂的“重要性”；if 条件也避免一概认定所有蜂类都数量庞大，与下一段提到蜂类受威胁保持一致。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>整体保护不等于等额投入</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

because every species is closely interconnected and is equally important, thus refuting the idea that certain animals should be prioritized

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

because species are closely interconnected, thus challenging the idea that only certain animals deserve protection

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

相互关联可以支持整体保护，却不能单独证明所有物种价值完全相同、资源投入必须相等。将争论对象改成“是否只有部分动物值得保护”，保留支持广泛保护的立场，同时允许结尾按风险优先分配资源，消除原文前后冲突。

</div>
</section>
