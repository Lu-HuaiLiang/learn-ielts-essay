</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

distinguish between A and B 是准确搭配；Some 与 may 则避免把所有年轻人都说成无法区分现实与虚构。后面的模仿情景也改成可能发生的例子，保留风险机制，不再把未经核实的印度犯罪案例写成事实。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>非必要条件不等于无害</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

watching such movies or playing such games is not the sine qua non for crimes

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Their enjoyment of fictional violence does not in itself establish a willingness to commit violence in real life.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

sine qua non 是“必要条件”，但某事不是犯罪的必要条件，并不能证明它无害。修改直接区分“欣赏虚构刺激”与“愿意现实施暴”；后面的社会原因也只提示不能草率归因，不能凭存在其他原因就排除娱乐内容的影响。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>教育效果须有内容条件</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

By watching violent entertainment, most people will be dissuaded from such behaviour

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

By watching entertainment that portrays the consequences of violence, some people may be dissuaded from such behaviour

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原句的 most people 与 will 保证了多数人的效果，论据不足。加入“呈现暴力后果”的内容条件，再用 some / may，因果链才清楚：看见伤害与后果→产生反思和共情→可能打消施暴意愿。

</div>
</section>
