# 高管是否应获得远高于普通员工的薪酬？

Executive Pay and Contribution

<div class="essay-meta">

C1 · [企业与工作](/topics/business-and-work) · [同意与否](/types/agree) · 440 words

</div>

<div class="custom-block info essay-question">
<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

Large companies should pay much higher salaries to CEOs and executives compared to other workers. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

</div>
</div>

<EssayReader essay-id="five-c1" :paragraphs="[&quot;One of the growing trends in modern society is that corporations place a premium on the role played by CEOs and accordingly they receive very high salaries; thus, a fierce debate rages over the growing salary disparities between them and other low-ranking workers. The salary for employees usually is determined by considering the contribution they make to the company or the responsibility they take on; therefore, executives can receive higher salaries and compensation in the form of stock. However, the real problem arises when they are paid much more than their performance warrants; their compensation should be performance-based. The gap can be particularly alarming when executives receive large rewards despite their companies' poor performance. Therefore, herein lies the question as to why the pay-for-performance model sometimes fails when it comes to executive salaries.&quot;,&quot;However, this belief overlooks the fact that the contribution made by junior employees is often assessed largely on the basis of their individual work, while the contribution made by a team leader should be assessed in terms of their role in improving the performance of the whole team. A good example would be a company whose CEO successfully restructures its operations, with higher pay representing recognition of that contribution.&quot;,&quot;Moreover, executives of failing companies retain the right to receive what they deserve even if companies are declining due to factors beyond their control. <h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>主体一｜公平反对意见</h3>

反方质疑的是报酬超过表现应得的程度，尤其企业业绩差却获得大奖励。performance-based给出评价原则，而非断言所有高管薪水都过高。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>薪酬看贡献责任</li>
<li>部分高管奖励过度</li>
<li>业绩差仍获大奖励</li>
<li>绩效原则受到质疑</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>主体二｜评价管理贡献的范围</h3>

普通员工往往按个人工作评价，领导者决策却影响全团队。成功重组公司是一个假设例子，说明高薪可承认管理者改善整体生产力的贡献。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>个人岗位贡献</li>
<li>管理影响全队</li>
<li>决策改善整体生产力</li>
<li>贡献规模可支持高薪</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">04</span>主体三｜合同工资与绩效奖金分开</h3>

公司失败可能有管理者无法控制的原因，人才也不保证成功。已经履职的约定工资应支付，但不能据此证明贡献不足时巨额绩效奖金合理。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>外部因素影响失败</li>
<li>优秀人才也不保证成功</li>
<li>工作应获约定工资</li>
<li>低贡献不支持巨额奖金</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">05</span>结尾｜回到责任与自身贡献</h3>

结尾用责任规模补充个人贡献标准；企业成败不单独决定薪水合理性，必须看高管本人的作用与职责。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>考虑个人贡献</li>
<li>考虑责任规模</li>
<li>不能只按公司成败</li>
<li>有条件认可高薪</li>
</ol>
</section>
