# 了解文化是否需要亲自旅行

Travel and Learning About Culture

<div class="essay-meta">

N08 · [旅游](/topics/tourism) · [同意与否](/types/agree) · 462 words

</div>

<div class="custom-block info essay-question">
<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

It is not necessary to travel to other places to learn about culture and people, because people can learn from books, films or the Internet. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

</div>
</div>

<details class="custom-block details essay-analysis study-panel">
<summary><h2 id="analysis" class="essay-section-title">解析</h2><span class="study-panel-caption">逐段梳理论证思路</span></summary>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>开头：限定替代程度</h3>

问题在于媒介能否替代了解文化所需的旅行，而非旅游是否重要；用cannot fully replace保留部分替代的空间。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>媒介文化资源增长</li>
<li>有人认为无需旅行</li>
<li>信息可替代一部分</li>
<li>直接体验不可完全替代</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>主体一：承认媒介优势</h3>

书影网络让人在家获取文化信息，节约交通住宿的时间和费用，因此对无需旅行的命题作程度性让步。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>旅行耗费时间金钱</li>
<li>媒介在家可访问</li>
<li>获得部分文化信息</li>
<li>节省旅行成本</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>主体二：文化亲历与附带经济效益</h3>

和当地人一起饮食过节带来描述难复现的学习；目的地收入另列为附带收益，不拿经济价值证明学习必要性。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>与当地人共同参与习俗</li>
<li>获得直接文化经验</li>
<li>文字描述难完全复现</li>
<li>游客消费另有经济收益</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">04</span>主体三：个人经历不可复制</h3>

计划、迷路、误车以及现实与介绍不符，形成旅行者自己的记忆；他人的叙述不能完整替代此种个人经历。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>计划旅行</li>
<li>经历意外与现实差异</li>
<li>形成个人理解</li>
<li>留下自己的记忆</li>
<li>媒介无法完整复现</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">05</span>结尾：知识与体验分开</h3>

媒介可探索世界，旅行可直接体验并加深理解；结尾不推出只有出行才能理解文化的绝对结论。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>媒介提供知识</li>
<li>旅行深化亲历理解</li>
<li>目的地另获经济收益</li>
</ol>
</section>

<details class="custom-block details essay-revisions study-panel">
<summary><h2 id="revisions" class="essay-section-title">小修说明</h2><span class="study-panel-caption">对照表达，理解修改原因</span></summary>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>回答能否替代亲历学习</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

However, it does not necessarily mean that tourism is of no importance.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

However, this does not necessarily mean that such resources can replace the firsthand learning that tourism provides.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

题目问“了解文化是否需要旅行”，而不是“旅行是否重要”。原句只证明重要性，还不能反驳可由媒介学习的观点。修改把反驳限定为亲历学习能否被替代，并在前段承认媒介可以提供部分文化知识；经济收益则保留为附带作用，不用它证明旅行的必要性。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>在家查阅的自然搭配</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

in their comfort at home

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

in the comfort of their own homes

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

表示“舒舒服服地待在自己家里”，自然搭配是 in the comfort of one’s own home。原来的 in their comfort at home 不是惯常表达；修正后仍是原有的“无需出门即可查阅资料”例子。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>信息传递与个人体验</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

books or the internet, a way to communicate travel information and share travel experiences, fails to make travel personal

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

books and the Internet, as ways to communicate travel information and share travel experiences, cannot fully reproduce such a personal experience

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原句用单数 a way 概括两类媒介，且 fails to make travel personal 把结论说得过于绝对。修改后的 and…as ways… 指代清楚，cannot fully reproduce 对应前面的个人经历：媒介可以传递信息，却不能完整重现旅行者自己的体验；不再推出“没旅行就无法理解文化”。

</div>
</section>
