<summary><h2 id="revisions" class="essay-section-title">小修说明</h2><span class="study-panel-caption">对照表达，理解修改原因</span></summary>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>access与结果分词并列</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the channels for customers to such food would be cut, thus encouraging them to purchase more healthy foods and drinks and mitigate the health problems that ensue from junk foods

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

customers’ access to these products would be restricted, thus encouraging them to purchase healthier foods and drinks and potentially mitigating the health problems associated with excessive consumption

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

用 access to 表明购买渠道受限；将 mitigating 与 encouraging 并列，避免误读成消费者既购买又直接治病，并保留健康效果的不确定性。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>预制类别不等于有害配料</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

despite knowing the dangers that lie in the ingredients (preservatives, sweeteners, or colour enhancers)

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

despite concerns about the nutritional quality of some products

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

不能把防腐剂、甜味剂等整个配料类别直接判为危险；改为对部分产品营养质量的担忧，并限定受禁令影响的食品，避免混淆预制与有害。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>结尾不得新增多数人适量断言</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

most of them consume such foods in moderation and thus the effort to improve declining public health by banning them at shops is far from viable because they are arguably necessary evils

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

the effort to improve public health by banning them at shops is not always viable, given consumers’ practical needs and the potential economic costs.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原结尾新增“多数人适度食用”这一未论证事实，也把食品笼统称为必要之恶；改为回扣正文的实际需求和经济影响，使理由与结论一致。

</div>
