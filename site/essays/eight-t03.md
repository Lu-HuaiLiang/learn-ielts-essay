# 全天免费公共交通

<div class="essay-meta">

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

Some people think the best way to solve traffic congestion in cities is to provide free public transport 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

The effort to offer free public transport is an ineffective way to ease traffic problems.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

The effort to offer free public transport may have a limited effect on traffic problems if services remain inconvenient.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

免费可能吸引部分乘客，不能因舒适便利也重要就判定无效；加上服务不便这一条件，使论证针对为何它未必是最佳办法。

</div>

<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>夜间与高峰错位及between搭配</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

However, it is bound to fail as traffic congestion often occurs during rush hours (e.g., between 8 am to 9 am).

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

However, extending services overnight may do little to relieve peak-hour congestion, as traffic congestion often occurs during rush hours (e.g., between 8 am and 9 am).

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

夜间增班与高峰拥堵错位，只能限制这部分措施的治堵效果，不能证明整个政策必败；同时修正 between … and … 的搭配。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>以成本效益回答best</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

In conclusion, unavailing and impractical, the 24/7 free policy is surely a recipe for failure, and hence other viable alternative strategies should be considered.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

In conclusion, although the 24/7 free policy could benefit some passengers, its limited appeal to some drivers and potentially high costs mean that it may be less cost-effective than targeted improvements to public transport and road bottlenecks.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

结论承认可能收益，再以吸引力和成本比较正文提出的替代方案，直接回应 best；不把“预算可能承压”夸大为必然超预算或失败。

</div>
