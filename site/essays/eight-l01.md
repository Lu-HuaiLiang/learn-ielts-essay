# 语言的主流化

The Rise of Dominant Languages

<div class="essay-meta">

L01 · [语言](/topics/language) · [积极／消极发展](/types/development) · 381 words

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

A few languages are increasingly spoken in different countries, while the usage of others is rapidly declining. Is this a positive or a negative development ?

<summary><h2 id="revisions" class="essay-section-title">小修说明</h2><span class="study-panel-caption">对照表达，理解修改原因</span></summary>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>贡献经济用contribute to</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the dominance of a few languages accrues to economic well-being.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

The dominance of a few languages contributes to economic well-being.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

accrue通常以利益作主语；这里要表达语言主流化促进经济福祉，用contribute to，并修正句首大写。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>新文化不抵消原文化损失</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

Nonetheless, this is far from the concern because while the culture created in the past is dying out, new cultures are being created:

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Nonetheless, although this loss remains a concern, new cultures are also being created:

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

新文化出现不能证明旧文化损失无需担忧；保留原来的文化融合论点，同时承认损失仍然存在。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>正负判断需要收益比较</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

The status quo can hardly be reversed due to the economic and scientific advantages; nonetheless, the necessary evil should also be considered: part of local culture will be lost.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

On balance, the economic and scientific advantages outweigh the cultural costs because they improve livelihoods and widen access to knowledge across borders; nonetheless, these costs should also be considered, since new cultural forms cannot fully replace the local traditions that may be lost.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

“趋势难以逆转”不能直接回答正面还是负面。结尾补出经济与知识共享收益更重要的比较依据，同时把必然文化损失改为可能性，保持原正面立场。

</div>
</section>
