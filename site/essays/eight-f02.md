# 提高高糖食品价格

Raising the Price of Sugary Products

<div class="essay-meta">

F02 · [饮食](/topics/food) · [同意与否](/types/agree) · 417 words

</div>

<div class="custom-block info essay-question">
<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

Many manufactured food and drink products contain high levels of sugar, which causes many health problems. Sugary products should be made more expensive to encourage people to consume less. Do you agree?

</div>
</div>

<li>涨价仍覆盖这些消费者</li>
<li>增加支出却未必改善其行为</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">04</span>替代：提供信息而非普遍加价</h3>

借包装警示原则提出糖含量分级标签，让过量消费者认识风险并有据选择；这是拟议方案而非未经核实的国家政策事实。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>清楚标示含糖量等级</li>
<li>消费者认识风险</li>
<li>更知情地选择与控制摄入</li>
<li>避免所有消费者成本上升</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">05</span>结尾：针对性判断</h3>

保留价格可能有用的让步，再回到已适量者成本与标签的信息作用；清楚表达偏好标签，未宣称标签保证效果。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>涨价或有健康作用</li>
<li>适量者仍承担成本</li>
<li>标签支持知情选择</li>
<li>偏好更有针对性的方案</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>价格用raised并保持条件结果一致</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

If the price of sugary products were to be made more expensive, people would be less likely to stick with them and reduce their consumption, which in turn lowers the risk of being prone to relevant health problems

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

If the prices of sugary products were to be raised, people might be less likely to buy them and might therefore reduce their consumption, which could in turn lower the risk of associated health problems.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

价格用 raised，商品才用 expensive；重复 might 明确“购买减少→摄入减少”，避免 less likely 同时支配 reduce，且使条件与结果语气一致。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>限定适量者与具体支出</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

While isolated examples can be found, people, by and large, eat high-sugar food in moderation and they only do it when they are under stress or heavy work before the deadline; they need such comfort food to bring them joy, warmth, and happiness.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

While cases of excessive consumption can be found, some people eat high-sugar food in moderation, for example when they are under stress or facing a heavy workload before a deadline; such comfort food can bring them joy, warmth, and happiness.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

删去“多数人如此”和“只在压力下吃”的无依据概括，修正 workload 搭配；随后补说明普遍涨价对已适量者的额外支出，形成具体反对理由。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>政策事实改成假设标签方案</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

For example, Singapore has imposed regulation that food and beverages should be labelled with different levels of sugar content using a government-developed grading system to curb the nation’s sugar intake.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

For example, food and beverages could be labelled with different levels of sugar content using a government-developed grading system to help consumers make informed choices.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原句将具体制度泛化到所有食品饮料，未核实不宜当事实引用；改为明确假设的标签方案，并删去香烟“从未禁售”的绝对断言，保留警示标签的类比功能。

</div>
