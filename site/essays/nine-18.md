<summary><h2 id="revisions" class="essay-section-title">小修说明</h2><span class="study-panel-caption">对照表达，理解修改原因</span></summary>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>提供经费而非购买金钱</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

should pay for the money to support the development

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

should provide funding to support the development

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

pay for 后面接所购买的商品或服务，不能把 money 当成购买对象。这里要表达政府提供经费，因此改成 provide funding；保留“社会受益→政府应资助”的原推进。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>共同出资与使用程度</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

health care and education are rather personal, it is intuitively unfair to use money from all to benefit very few

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

they view health care and education as more personal; from this perspective, it seems unfair to use money from all to pay for services that some people use much more than others

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原句既用逗号连接两个独立分句，也把教育医疗说成只有极少数人受益。改用分号，并把反方的公平担忧落在“个人使用程度不同，却共同出资”上，仍保留原来的个人受益论证。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>允许额外私人服务</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

guarantee that few people could have better access

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

allow those who wish to pay for additional services to do so

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

few 表示“几乎没有人”，与原文允许私人选择的本意相反。改成允许有意愿的人购买额外服务，并在前文明确公共服务保障的是 basic services，公共保障与私人选择才不冲突。

</div>
</section>
