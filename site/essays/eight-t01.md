# 提高油价缓解拥堵

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>

Some people think the best way to solve traffic congestion is to increase the cost of fuel for cars. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

用缺少合适替代这一条件限制涨价效果，再以公交网络和道路设计对应替代方案，回答为什么不是最佳。

<li>改善公交与路网设计</li>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>可行替代方式的行为前提</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

private vehicles are much more affordable for people to travel around

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

drivers have practical alternatives to private vehicles

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原句比较对象缺失，且与后文私家车成本较高不一致；改为检查有无可行替代方式，使反驳与开头的出行刚需衔接。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>自愿换乘的主语与可能性</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

shifting mode from private vehicles to public transport voluntarily takes place

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

people may voluntarily shift from private vehicles to public transport

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

以 people 作主语，让“自愿”明确修饰人的选择；may 保留服务改善可能促成换乘的论证，不把行为变化写成必然。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>条件效果替代必败判断</h3>

is ineffective in view of the flawed assumption

may have limited effects where drivers lack suitable alternatives

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

“成本以外还有因素”不能推出涨价完全无效；结论限定适用条件，并与前文承认其可能减流量保持一致。
