<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>间接疑问句与角色搭配</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

what role should males and females take in societies

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

what roles males and females should play in society

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

as to 后接间接疑问从句，要用陈述语序 males and females should，而非 should males and females。表达在社会中的角色通常用 play a role in society；两性承担多种角色，这里用 roles。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>按个人能力评估适岗性</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

Therefore, women, who are generally less sturdy and more fragile, are not the optimal choice for these occupations.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Therefore, applicants who do not meet the relevant standards, regardless of gender, are not suitable for these particular duties; this does not establish that women as a group are unsuitable.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

岗位需要体能，并不能推出所有女性都不适合。原文由群体印象跳到整类人的适岗结论；修改仍承认高体能岗位的门槛，但把筛选单位改为个人、标准改为具体职责，从而与全文支持女性参与的立场一致。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>can play与already play</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

females can and are playing a central role in policing and troops

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

females can play, and already play, a central role in policing and the armed forces

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

can 后要接 play，are 后才接 playing，原来的两个助动词不能共同支配 playing。policing 是工作而 troops 是人员，改为 policing and the armed forces，使并列对象清楚。保留“有能力参与且已在参与”的双重含义。

</div>
