# 电子游戏的利弊

The Benefits and Risks of Video Games

<div class="essay-meta">

N09 · [游戏](/topics/games) · [利弊权衡](/types/outweigh) · 335 words

</div>

<div class="custom-block info essay-question">
<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

Some people regard video games as harmless fun, or even as a useful educational tool. Others, however, believe that video games are having an adverse effect on the people who play them. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

</div>
</div>

Prolonged inactivity may contribute to obesity, while excessive gaming may also interfere with their concentration on other tasks.&quot;,&quot;However, recognising such risks does not necessarily diminish their value. They can be harmless fun when played in moderation, because physical problems and a sedentary lifestyle are more closely linked to excessive use than to playing games per se. In addition, gamers, when operating gaming controls, can develop good hand-eye coordination and sharpen skills like decision-making, anticipation, and resource management. For example, when users play a game set in a historical period, they may be taken on a trip down historical memory lane, as the game can introduce them to historical events and figures, although fictional elements need to be distinguished from historical facts.&quot;,&quot;In conclusion, video games on balance afford people valuable opportunities to learn and have fun, while many of their risks can be reduced by choosing suitable content and limiting playing time.
