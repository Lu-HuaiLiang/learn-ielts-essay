<div class="essay-meta">

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

Some people think teaching children of different abilities together benefits everyone. Others think intelligent children should be taught separately and given special treatment. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>分组理由：不同速度的适配</h3>

混班教师需同时应对广泛需求；分组使学得快者继续推进、需要帮助者获得更合适解释，关键是双方都得到匹配。

</ol>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">04</span>个人观点：公平条件与可变分组</h3>

加速教学需同时保障平等资源、允许流动，防止固定标签；学生其他背景仍多样，分组不等于消灭所有多样性。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
