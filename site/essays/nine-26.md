科学家也稀少且长期投入，农业研究提高产量可改善粮食供给；科研压力亦重大，所以这些特质不足单独证明运动员应获远高报酬。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>科研者也有稀缺才能与长期投入</li>
<li>提高产量</li>
<li>改善粮食供给</li>
<li>承担重大压力责任</li>
<li>相同奖励原则应适用于其他职业</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">05</span>结尾：修正立场冲突</h3>

用excessive与社会贡献比较重申反对，删去原来误贴的fully justifiable；不是否认任何运动员收入都可支付。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>贡献责任应作为标准</li>
<li>才能投入压力不足单独证明巨大差距</li>
<li>相对其他职业体育薪资过高</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>薪水与薪水作比较</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

They are overpaid especially when comparing these super salaries with those of top surgeons

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Their salaries appear excessive when compared with those of top surgeons

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

when comparing 的逻辑主语原本会落到 They，即运动员自己在比较。改成 Their salaries…when compared，把比较对象统一为“薪水与薪水”，也清楚呈现本段反方的评价尺度。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>假设研发例子取代未核验史实</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the scientist Yuan Longping, developed the first hybrid rice, boosting food security and providing a robust food source

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

an agricultural scientist could develop higher-yielding rice, improving food security and providing a more reliable food supply

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

主谓之间原来的逗号应删除；“首个杂交稻”的科学史断言在材料中没有核验。改为农业科学家的条件性例子，保留“长期研发→提高产量→改善粮食供给”的原论证功能，food supply 也比 robust food source 更准确。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>结尾与反对高薪立场统一</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the amount of money sports stars make is fully justifiable

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

the amount of money sports stars make is excessive when compared with the rewards received by other professionals on the basis of their social contribution

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

fully justifiable 支持高薪，与本篇反对立场直接冲突。改回 excessive，并明确依据是社会贡献与其他职业报酬的比较；同时正文说明稀缺技能、投入和压力也存在于其他职业，因此不能仅靠这些理由证明体育高薪合理。

</div>
</section>
