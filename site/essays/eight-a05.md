# 强调新特征

Advertising New Product Features

<div class="essay-meta">

A05 · [广告](/topics/advertising) · [混合／直接问答](/types/mixed) · 406 words

</div>

<div class="custom-block info essay-question">
<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

In their advertising, businesses nowadays usually emphasise that their products are new in some way. Why is this? Do you think it is a positive or negative development?

</div>
</div>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>逗号拼接与get hands on</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

A good example can be found in the smartphone industry, where the new iPhone was released with its highlighted function – dynamic island, numerous people camped outside the shops to get their hand at the most desirable iPhone.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

A good example can be found in the smartphone industry, where a new iPhone advertised with a highlighted function such as Dynamic Island may encourage consumers to get their hands on the latest model.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原句用逗号拼接完整分句，get their hand at 也不正确。保留产品功能例子，改成可能的购买反应，避免把无来源排队事件当证据。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>满足需求的搭配与竞争对象</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

markets may claim their products distinguish themselves from the competition because they can suit the needs that their counterparts cannot achieve

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

marketers may claim their products distinguish themselves from the competition because they can meet needs that their counterparts cannot satisfy

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

markets 是市场，marketers 才是营销人员；满足需求应为 meet/satisfy needs，不能用 achieve needs。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>总体负面补具体财务理由</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

Such anxiety-creating and function-exaggerating advertising takes the lion’s share in the market, predisposing consumers to the acquisition of goods or services that are beyond their real needs. Therefore, it on balance produces a negative outcome.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Such anxiety-creating and function-exaggerating advertising can predispose consumers to the acquisition of goods or services that are beyond their real needs. Therefore, on balance, I regard this trend as negative, because encouraging repeated purchases without substantial improvements places an unnecessary financial burden on consumers.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

去掉未经支持的最大市场份额断言；总体负面评价改以缺乏实质改进却反复购买造成的额外开支为依据，使利弊权衡更清楚。

</div>
</section>
