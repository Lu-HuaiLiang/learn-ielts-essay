<h3><span class="analysis-index">04</span>观点兼收尾：路径因人而异</h3>

大学与职业培训各服务不同目标；进一步扩展成功到健康和人际关系，从定义层面说明不能把大学当普遍最佳途径。

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>职业资格例子分类一致</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

Occupations such as lawyers and doctors are only exclusive to those with postgraduate qualifications.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Professions such as law and medicine often require relevant academic and professional qualifications.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

lawyers/doctors是人，不能直接作occupations的同类例子；删除only exclusive的重复，也不再把研究生学历说成这些职业一概适用的准入要求。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>财务优势不等于人生全面成功</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

This makes them more successful than those taking up low-paid occupations

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

This can put them in a stronger financial position than those taking up low-paid occupations

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

薪资优势只能支持财务维度，不能直接推出整体人生更成功；改后与结尾纳入健康、人际关系的成功定义一致。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>职业培训与特定岗位匹配</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

By contrast, those who have failed to go to college but receive vocational training are more likely to be successful:

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

By contrast, those who do not go to college but receive vocational training may be better prepared for certain occupations than graduates of academic courses:

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

不把未上大学预设成失败，也不笼统断言职教更成功；明确比较对象和特定职业的准备程度，才能回应大学是否为最佳路径。

</div>
