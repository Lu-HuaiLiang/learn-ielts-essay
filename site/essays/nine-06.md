# 国际旅游兴起的原因与影响

Why International Tourism Is Growing

<div class="essay-meta">

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>

Many people today are choosing to travel to other countries. Why is this happening? Is this a positive or negative development?

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>中产群体扩大</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the number of the middle class proliferates

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

the middle class is expanding

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

the middle class 指一个社会群体，the number of the middle class 不自然；proliferate 也不适合直接与这里的 number 搭配。is expanding 保留“中产群体扩大→更多人拥有可用于旅行的收入”的原因链，后文也明确写 more people，避免 them 指代含糊。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>from A to B范围结构</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

ranging from what people usually eat and how people celebrate the local festivals

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

ranging from what people usually eat to how people celebrate local festivals

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

范围结构应为 ranging from A to B。A、B 继续保留两个名词性从句，因此长句形式没有被拆掉，只修正连接关系；local festivals 泛指当地节日，不需要 the。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>倒装中的复数主语</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

With this boom also comes problems.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

With this boom also come problems.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

这是完全倒装，真正的主语是后面的复数 problems，因此谓语用 come。下文还把已有国家禁令改为可采取的一般措施，并补上限制旅游设施毁林这一动作，使政府措施直接回应本段的栖息地损失问题。

</div>
</section>
