适者生存不等于只有最强壮的动物存活；better-adapted 对应“更适应环境”，more likely 也避免必然性。段首增加 Critics argue、后句用 They believe，是为了把这套反对保护的推理清楚归给反方，随后才能用人为破坏作回应。

</div>

<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

If the money allocated to animal conservation were to be used for other concerns, it would bring trivial changes in other aspects

<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

If a relatively small conservation budget were to be used for other concerns, it might bring only limited improvements in other areas
