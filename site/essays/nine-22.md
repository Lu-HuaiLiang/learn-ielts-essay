# 全球合作

International Cooperation and Local Action

<div class="essay-meta">

Many people feel that most of the urgent problems can only be solved by international cooperation. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

</div>

<li>跨国协调政策</li>
<li>各国分工减排与遏制毁林</li>
<li>共同措施更有效</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>主体二：因地制宜与独立行动</h3>

车少地区换电车即时效果可能有限，需调整重点；塑料堵排水的紧急本地问题可以不等协议就限袋、收垃圾，真正回应only。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>国情不同</li>
<li>统一措施即时效果有别</li>
<li>塑料堵塞本地排水</li>
<li>独立限袋与改善收集</li>
<li>无需等待国际协议</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">04</span>结尾：尺度决定分工</h3>

国际合作提供全球框架，本地既执行协议也独立处理区域问题；一个本地例子只支持保留范围，不能自动证明多数问题不需合作。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>全球挑战需要合作</li>
<li>本地落实具体措施</li>
<li>部分区域问题独立处理</li>
<li>相对重要性随尺度变化</li>
</ol>
</section>

<summary><h2 id="revisions" class="essay-section-title">小修说明</h2><span class="study-panel-caption">对照表达，理解修改原因</span></summary>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>So...that倒装与责任范围</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

So interconnected is the world, that no country can evade the responsibility to mitigate global problems because every country contributes to the problem, albeit to varying degrees.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

So interconnected is the world that no country can evade responsibility for mitigating shared global problems to which it contributes, albeit to varying degrees.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

So + 形容词 + be + 主语 + that 是可保留的正式倒装，that 前不应加这个逗号。responsibility for doing 更自然；to which it contributes 也把责任限定在该国参与造成的共同问题上，避免每个国家都造成所有全球问题的过强概括。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>效果有限不等于不可行</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

for underdevelopment countries, such a measure is far from viable because few people own their private vehicles

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

where private vehicle ownership is low, replacing existing cars may have limited immediate effects

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

underdevelopment 是名词，不能这样修饰 countries；更重要的是，私家车少只能说明替换车辆的即时影响可能有限，不能直接证明政策不可行。改成条件场景，保留不同国情需要不同政策重点的原论证。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>独立本地行动回应only</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

Tanzania made great progress in controlling the use of plastic bags while making little contribution to reducing carbon emissions

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

where plastic waste blocks local drains and increases the risk of flooding, a local government could restrict plastic bags and improve waste collection without waiting for an international agreement

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

未经核验的坦桑尼亚对比不能充分回应 only。保留塑料污染例子，改为本地可以独立处理的紧急排水问题：无需等待国际协议就能采取措施。这样才能说明部分区域问题不以国际合作为前提，而不只是说国际协议还需要本地执行。

</div>
</section>
