<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>

<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>表达之后补受众理解</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

what experiences such as joy or loss feel like from the inside

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

把“艺术受欢迎的原因”拉回题目所问的“艺术告诉我们什么”。self-expression 后补出读者能获得的理解：what experiences such as joy or loss feel like from the inside。这样表达、作品、受众的理解形成一条因果链，而不只是说上艺术课有助放松。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>修正词性并限制艺术科学对立</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

art brings aesthetics while science brings functional

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

reveals the aesthetic value of everyday life

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

修好词性与搭配，但保留对照句。原文 art brings aesthetics while science brings functional 中 functional 是形容词，不能这样作宾语；修订用 aesthetic value 与 how things function 分别说明艺术和科学的侧重点，并用 alone 避免断言科学与审美毫无关联。unique characters 改为 distinctive character，表示一个时期的整体特征。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>撤去未经支持的人物因果</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

not only on its operating system but also on its industrial design

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原稿把乔布斯上书法课与 iPhone 的大小写命名直接联系起来，材料没有支持这条因果。这里保留“手机技术＋工业设计”的例子，改成一般性的 may rest … not only … but also …；结尾也直接总结情感、文化经验、审美价值，替换未在前文论证过的“维持现状”。

</div>
</section>
