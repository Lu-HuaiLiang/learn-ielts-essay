# 未成年人与成年人是否同罚

Sentencing Young and Adult Offenders

<div class="essay-meta">

C02 · [犯罪](/topics/crime) · [同意与否](/types/agree) · 396 words

</div>

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

Young people who commit serious crimes, such as a robbery or a violent attack should be published in the same way as adults. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

</div>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>proportionate与威慑限定</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

The argument for an equal punishment policy is that proportionated punishment can guarantee crime prevention.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

The argument for an equal punishment policy is that proportionate punishment can help deter crime.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

相称的形容词应为proportionate；刑罚可能有威慑作用，但不能由此保证完全预防犯罪。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>外部因素到判断受损的条件</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

In this case, delinquencies are in effect attributable largely to extrinsic factors, and thus it is sensible that punishments can be mitigated thoughtfully and duly.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Where such influences have impaired a young offender's judgment, it may be reasonable to take them into account and mitigate punishment thoughtfully and duly.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

外部因素存在并不自动免除或减轻责任；补上判断能力受到影响这一中间条件，使“考虑从轻”有明确论证依据。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>个别评估替代未核实法律概括</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

Developed countries such as America have adopted a law called “malice supplies the want of years”, whereby juvenile offenders should be individually assessed as to whether they are as mature and culpable as the typical adult violent offenders.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Juvenile offenders should be individually assessed to determine their level of maturity and culpability, rather than judged on age alone.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

去掉无出处的现行法律概括，保留成熟度与责任应个别评估的主张；后句仍允许严重犯罪受到较重处罚，但不直接推出终身监禁或死刑。

</div>
