# 更长刑期是否最能减少犯罪

Longer Sentences and Crime Prevention

<div class="essay-meta">

C03 · [犯罪](/topics/crime) · [双边讨论](/types/discussion) · 405 words

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

Some people think the best way of reducing crime is to give longer prison sentences. Others, however, think there are other better ways to reduce crime. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">05</span>结尾：以犯罪前干预比较</h3>

承认更长刑期对部分人有威慑，但替代方法在犯罪发生前处理内在动机和外部影响，因此提出长期更有效的比较判断。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>长刑期威慑部分人</li>
<li>单靠刑期无法涵盖全部原因</li>
<li>教育榜样监管前置干预</li>
<li>长期方案更全面</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>威慑只适用于部分理性决策者</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

If this policy were to be adopted, the deterrent effect would suffice to prevent crimes: those who would otherwise be prone to robbery may stop, just simply because of such severe punishment.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

If this policy were to be adopted, the deterrent effect could help prevent some crimes: those who would otherwise be prone to robbery might refrain from offending because of such severe punishment.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

保留较长刑期可能有用的论点，将足以预防一切犯罪降为帮助减少部分犯罪；might与假设语气衔接，并明确停止的是犯罪行为。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>教育须说明改变什么动机</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

In effect, criminals hardly, if all at all, think about the consequences but always regard themselves are the ones who are lucky enough to escape justice.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

In effect, some offenders give little thought to the consequences or regard themselves as people who are likely to escape justice.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

修正regard…as结构并去掉所有罪犯“总是如此”的概括；后续新增道德判断与冲突解决机制，说明教育为何可能比单纯加刑更有效。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>用犯罪前预防解释长期比较</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

In conclusion, even though longer prison sentences may have a stronger deterrent effect, it is a futile attempt to reduce crime.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

In conclusion, even though longer prison sentences may have a stronger deterrent effect on some potential offenders, they may be insufficient on their own.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

有一定威慑作用与完全徒劳互相矛盾，改为单独使用可能不足；末句再按预防动机和外部影响进行比较，不能仅因还有其他因素就否定其最有效性。

</div>
