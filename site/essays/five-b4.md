# 新城镇是否应多建公园和体育设施、少建商场？

<div class="essay-meta">

B4 · [建筑与城市](/topics/buildings-and-cities) · [同意与否](/types/agree) · 374 words

</div>

<div class="custom-block info essay-question">
<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

</div>

Moreover, parks are ideal places for the members of a community to assemble, socialize and interact, which could not only build a sense of community but also contribute to their mental well-being.
