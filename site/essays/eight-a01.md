# 禁止儿童广告

<div class="essay-meta">

<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>benefits后关系从句补that</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the benefits accrue to companies and children

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

the benefits that accrue to companies and children

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

lies in 后需要名词短语；补 that 后，accrue 所在从句才能修饰 benefits。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>肥胖论证补过量摄入环节</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

which has given rise to the childhood obesity epidemic worldwide

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

a pattern that may encourage excessive consumption of high-calorie foods and contribute to childhood obesity

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原句直接把全球儿童肥胖归因于广告，跨度过大。补出过量摄入的中间环节，并用 may、contribute to 保留论证而避免确证强因果。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>误导广告与促销限制范围</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Such problems justify a ban on misleading food advertisements aimed at children, alongside stringent restrictions on promotional tactics that encourage unhealthy eating.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

明确禁令针对误导性食品广告，并区分禁令与促销限制，避免从赠品吸引力直接跳到全面禁广告；未经核实的具体法规改为政策主张。
