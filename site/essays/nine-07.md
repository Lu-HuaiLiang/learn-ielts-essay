# 国际旅游的利弊权衡

Weighing International Tourism

<div class="essay-meta">

N07 · [旅游](/topics/tourism) · [利弊权衡](/types/outweigh) · 430 words

</div>

<div class="custom-block info essay-question">
<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

International tourism has brought enormous benefits to many places. At the same time, there is some concern about its impact on local inhabitants and the environment. Do the disadvantages outweigh the advantages?

</div>
</div>

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>设施扩展</li>
<li>毁林建设</li>
<li>栖息地损失</li>
<li>限制开发与减少游客伤害</li>
<li>剩余损害降低</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">04</span>主体三：文化风险可转为保护动力</h3>

迎合游客可能商业化传统；若本地人经营旅游并把收入用于习俗，旅游本身可成为文化保护载体。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>迎合外来需求</li>
<li>传统可能弱化</li>
<li>政策重视文化</li>
<li>本地社区经营并回投收入</li>
<li>文化认同增强</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="paragraph-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">05</span>结尾：比较剩余损害与持续收益</h3>

只在保护措施限制损害时支持旅游，比较的是持续生计和文化保存收益与控制后的损害，不是单纯数优点。

<ol class="argument-chain" aria-label="论证步骤">
<li>监管和各方努力</li>
<li>环境文化损害有限</li>
<li>持续生计与文化收益</li>
<li>收益超过剩余损害</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>恢复句界与让步主句</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the environment t on balance yields positive outcomes in the form of economic prosperity and cultural preservation. Despite some potential environmental and cultural issues.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

the environment. On balance, it yields positive outcomes in the form of economic prosperity and cultural preservation, despite some potential environmental and cultural issues.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原文在 environment 后漏了清楚的句界和主语，Despite 短语又单独成了残句。修改补出 On balance, it…，并将 despite 接回完整主句，使“存在风险，但总体利大于弊”的原立场在引言中明确成立。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>旅游并非唯一发展途径</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the opportunities only come with being part of international tourism

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

participation in international tourism can bring valuable employment opportunities

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

only 把旅游写成发展机会的唯一来源，前文没有证明这一点。改为 can bring 仍保留“游客带来收入与岗位”的论证，但不排除其他产业；原来的 greatest need 也相应收窄，避免把旅游收益直接变成所有发展中经济体都最需要旅游。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">03</span>负面影响与旅游的主语分开</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

the adverse effects on local culture, with policies that place a premium on cultural histories, local heritage sites, and customs, can be mitigated or even transformed into a vehicle for preserving and revitalizing local cultures

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

with policies that place a premium on cultural histories, local heritage sites, and customs, the adverse effects on local culture can be mitigated and tourism itself can become a vehicle for preserving and revitalizing local cultures

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

可以被缓解的是 adverse effects；能成为文化保护载体的是 tourism。原句让“负面影响”直接变成“载体”，逻辑主语错位。现在保留长句与 place a premium on、a vehicle for 等表达，通过两个清楚的主谓结构把“减轻风险”和“用旅游支持文化”接起来。

</div>
</section>
