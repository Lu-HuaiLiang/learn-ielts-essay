# 刑满释放后再次犯罪

Reducing Reoffending after Release

<div class="essay-meta">

C04 · [犯罪](/topics/crime) · [原因与措施](/types/causes) · 370 words

</div>

<div class="custom-block info essay-question">
<h2 id="question" class="essay-section-title custom-block-title">题目</h2>
<div lang="en">

Some ex-prisoners commit crimes after being released from the prison. What do you think is the cause? How can it be solved?

</div>
</div>

<EssayReader essay-id="eight-c04" :paragraphs="[&quot;One of the worrying issues in modern society is that some ex-convicts, despite knowing the possible consequences, repeat their illegal behaviour. Such a situation may increase the risk of reoffending as a means of livelihood.&quot;,&quot;Another factor that may prompt them to relapse is society’s attitudes toward convicted criminals. Such exclusion may limit their opportunities to rebuild their lives and increase the risk of reoffending.&quot;,&quot;By addressing the underlying causes, policymakers can help reduce recidivism with educational and employment programs. Work programs can also be administered while participants are in prison to provide them with experience and skills that increase their employability. If adequately supported, these two types of programs can have far-reaching effects: they can provide a stabilizing routine for ex-convicts, occupying the time that might otherwise be used for illegal activities; negative perceptions of ex-prisoners may gradually weaken as they earn a steady income and start to build relationships with others while working.&quot;,&quot;In conclusion, recidivism may arise when ex-prisoners fail to secure a job and gain support from others. Efforts should be made to change the status quo, including implementing education and employment programs, both of which can increase employability and thus contribute to rehabilitation.&quot;]">

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>employers与假设经历明确</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

After having been incarcerated for a long time, they suffer from a lack of working experience and job training, which are usually what the employees are looking for; for example, a 21-year-old receives a 10-year sentence and how can he compete with his non-criminal peers?

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

After having been incarcerated for a long time, they may lack recent work experience and job training, which are usually what employers are looking for; for example, if a 21-year-old receives a 10-year sentence without access to suitable training, he may struggle to compete with his non-criminal peers after release.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

招聘方是employers而非employees；把反问改成有条件的假设，说明缺少近期经验和培训如何阻碍就业，而非声称所有服刑者都无法竞争。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>处理原因的逻辑主语</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

Understanding the underlying causes, recidivism can be reduced with educational and employment programs.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

By addressing the underlying causes, policymakers can help reduce recidivism with educational and employment programs.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

原句分词的逻辑主语误落到recidivism上；明确由政策制定者处理原因，并把“理解原因”推进为采取措施。

</div>
</section>
