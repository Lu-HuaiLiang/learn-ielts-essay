<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">01</span>宾语从句语序与必要性范围</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

some people think no longer should students learn foreign languages.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

some people think that students no longer need to learn foreign languages.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

think后的宾语从句采用陈述语序；用no longer need回应题面“不必学习”，避免把“不必要”升级成“不应该”。

</div>
</section>

<section class="revision-analysis">
<h3><span class="analysis-index">02</span>机会成本属于反方观点</h3>
<div class="revision-comparison">
<div class="revision-before"><span class="revision-label">原表达</span><div lang="en">

The time that would otherwise be wasted on such learning could be better used for other subjects.

</div></div>
<div class="revision-after"><span class="revision-label">修订表达</span><div lang="en">

Supporters of this view therefore argue that the time that would otherwise be spent on such learning could be better used for other subjects.

</div></div>
</div>
<div class="revision-reason"><span class="revision-label">修改原因</span>

继续使用翻译工具不能证明学语言是浪费；spent保留机会成本比较，并明确这是反方观点，避免与作者支持学习的立场混淆。

</div>
</section>
