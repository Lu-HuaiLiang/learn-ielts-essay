# 气候变化 Global warming

## 第01篇 气候变化 适应与预防 335词

**原题**

Some people think instead of preventing climate change, we need to find a way to live with it. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**本篇调整说明**：保留“适应与预防并重”的立场；将预防目标限定为限制进一步升温和损害，并将未核实的洛杉矶实例改为一般措施。

There has been a fierce yet unresolved debate as to how to deal with climate change. With the difficulty in tackling the changes to weather patterns on a global scale comes a belief that efforts should be made, not to prevent **further warming,** but to live with **climate change.** Although efforts should be made to adapt to the current situation, the importance of prevention **should not** be diminished.

The argument in favour of coexisting with climate change is that such a problem cannot be **resolved** in a short time. There are many contributors to **climate** change, such as **fossil-fuel** consumption, **industrial** emissions, and population growth, among others, some of which are **difficult** to stop completely. As a result, a **sensible** way to alleviate **its effects** is to **adapt** to such a worrying situation. **For** example, **residents** and developers **can** heatproof **buildings** by planting large trees that offer shade and putting plants and gardens on top of roofs **to** help **reduce heat entering buildings. Streets can also be covered** with light-coloured materials that reflect rather than absorb **sunlight,** so they remain cooler than typical black roads. Therefore, learning to live with this problem is viable and necessary.

However, reaching such a conclusion does not necessarily diminish the importance of prevention. **Growth in** energy **consumption** can be restricted **through greater efficiency** in a way that **supports** economic development while slowing down the **rate** at which fossil fuels are being burned; meanwhile, efforts are being made to develop alternative energy **sources** (e.g., solar power and wind energy), which **can reduce reliance** on **fossil fuels. Completely halting** climate change is impossible in a short time, but **efforts** to **limit further warming** should never be underestimated. **Such** progress, however minimal it is, can ultimately help us to achieve our goal: **preventing further damage from** climate **change.**

In conclusion, although efforts should be made to adapt to the current situation, the achievement of sustainable development necessitates **efforts to prevent further warming.** Only with both adaptation and prevention can society truly **benefit.**

**最值得理解的三处调整**

**1．** 原文：

> a more sensible way to alleviate the problem is to be accustomed to such a worrying situation

修改后：

> a sensible way to alleviate its effects is to adapt to such a worrying situation

be accustomed to 只是“习惯于”，不能解释损害为何减少；adapt to 才是采取适应措施。把 problem 改为 its effects，也区分了“减轻高温带来的影响”与“阻止气候变化本身”，与后面的遮阴、屋顶绿化和浅色路面形成因果链。

**2．** 原文：

> putting plants and gardens on top of roofs that use vegetation to help trap heat

修改后：

> putting plants and gardens on top of roofs to help reduce heat entering buildings

原来的 that use vegetation 修饰 roofs，既生硬，又把降温机制写成“困住热量”。改为不定式说明种植的目的：减少进入建筑物的热量；这样保留绿化例子，同时让措施与降温结果一致。

**3．** 原文：

> Prevention of climate change is impossible in a short time, but attempting to do such a try should never be underestimated.

修改后：

> Completely halting climate change is impossible in a short time, but efforts to limit further warming should never be underestimated.

do such a try 搭配不成立；更关键的是，全文已经承认气候变化发生，不能再以“让它从未发生”为目标。completely halting 与 limit further warming 区分“短期彻底停止”及“限制进一步升温”，所以前者困难并不否定后者的价值。

**原题**

In modern society, it is no longer necessary to slaughter animals for producing food, or non-food products such as clothes or medicine. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

The killing and slaughter of animals for food, research and product testing have **been** sanctioned by long usage; however, with **advances in modern society** comes a belief that such behaviour should cease forthwith. Even though animals have a prima facie right to life, **some uses** of animals **remain necessary and** can be morally justified, **while certain practices are** unacceptable.

The argument against such exploitation for food is that animals deserve **a** prima facie right to life, which **could** be overridden by **human** survival **needs** in the past due to the scarcity of food resources. Agriculture has thrived in recent years, making it possible for **many** people to **rely** on **plant-based foods** to acquire the necessary **protein** and **other nutrients.** However, it is not realistic to **assume** that all people **can** stop eating meat and **rely** solely **on plant-based foods immediately,** nor **can we assume that** the world population **currently has access to nutritionally adequate alternatives.**

The same principles also apply to medical research. The use of animals in **testing** can be morally acceptable, given that **developing** and **testing** a vaccine for a lethal human **disease may require** animal **studies where validated alternatives are unavailable. In such cases, the practice can** be **morally** justified, **provided that** these animals used in experimentation are humanely treated.

However, **when** it comes to non-food products such as clothes, **it** is worthwhile to point out that **alternatives** to animals’ skins and hides, **including** man-made **fibres such as** rayon, already exist and **can be produced on a large** scale, thus making it unnecessary to kill animals **solely** for clothes. However, the skins and hides that are produced by **the meat-packing** industry should be fully used. Nonetheless, some species are hunted and killed solely for their skins and furs, including lizards, foxes, and crocodiles, among others, which **justifies a** strict prohibition on such slaughter.

In conclusion, **slaughtering** animals to produce food and medicine **can be justified where it remains necessary,** while a total ban on **hunting animals solely** for **their skins and furs** to make clothes should be imposed.

## 第03篇 动物保护是否投入过多 349词

**原题**

Some people claim that too much focus and resources have been spent on protecting wild animals and birds. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**本篇调整说明**：保留反驳“保护投入过多”的立场；将预算已经合理分配、挪用保护资金必无帮助等断言改为有限条件下的比较。

The answer is unequivocal as to the importance of protecting animals; however, some people argue that undue emphasis is put on the safeguarding of various species of wild animals and birds. Although such efforts may seem unnecessary, less effort has been made than **is needed** to prevent animals from dying out.

The argument against current conservation initiatives is that they are an unnecessary waste of valuable resources. **Critics argue that the** natural development of ecosystems involves survival of the fittest, whereby **better-adapted** animals **are more likely** to **survive** while some **species** go extinct. **They believe that this** should not be interrupted by **human** intervention arising from the seeming kindness and care for certain animals. However, **much of the current reduction in biodiversity** is **associated with** human activities **rather than natural processes alone.** The **populations** of some **species,** such as cheetahs and snow **leopards, have declined, putting them at risk of extinction.** Such problems, in fact, strongly correlate **with** human activities such as commercial logging, **urban** sprawl, and poaching, among others, thus necessitating **conservation efforts to reduce this damage.**

Moreover, opponents of animal protection claim that with too much focus on such conservation comes an economic burden. **Many** endangered **species** are **found** in developing nations in Africa, Southeast Asia, and South America. National governments in these countries have a variety of other concerns related to healthcare, education, and employment, among others. The money **spent** on animal conservation could otherwise be **used** to **address these human needs.** However, **the** government budget **can be allocated** to address **both conservation and human welfare, rather than treating them** as **mutually exclusive.** If **a relatively small** conservation **budget** were to be used for other concerns, it **might** bring **only limited improvements** in other **areas while failing** to mitigate the problem of **declining wildlife populations,** a problem that **can have** far-reaching effects on **humans through damage to ecosystems.**

In conclusion, despite the financial cost, it is essential to protect wild animals which may go extinct. **More attention to** animal protection is **still needed;** governments should continue to take action before it is too **little,** too **late.**

## 第04篇 保护所有动物还是部分动物 381词

**原题**

Many believe that it is important to protect all wild animals, while others think that it is important to protect some, not all of them. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

**本篇调整说明**：区分“所有动物都值得保护”与“所有动物必须获得同等投入”；将蜂类经食物链影响捕食者的例子改为假设性机制，保留原因果方向。

The answer is unequivocal as to the importance of protecting animals; however, **some** have suggested that the protection of wild animals should be selective, whereas others believe that all wild animals deserve attention. Both views are discussed here to seek a **conclusion.**

The argument in favour of safeguarding certain wild animals is that they should **be** distinguished from other species by the **threats they** face and **their value.** For instance, **unlike** animals such as rats and pigeons, which have adapted **successfully** to urban environments, tigers are **endangered** wild **animals** and require intensive conservation efforts. Therefore, **there is** insufficient **justification for** providing equal support to species in different circumstances. Moreover, **advocates of selective protection regard** some animals **as** more deserving of preservation. The tiger, to continue the previous example, is greatly valued for being elegant and for being rare, while **animals** such as **bees** are valuable not for their beauty but for their usefulness to humanity. However, the **urgency** of **protecting a particular bee species may be lower if it has a** large **and stable population.**

However, all **wild** animals **deserve protection from human harm,** as humanity has been **a** catalyst for **the endangerment of many species.** The **populations** of some **species, including** cheetahs, snow leopards and **certain bee species, have declined, raising concerns about their survival.** Such problems, in fact, strongly correlate **with** human activities such as commercial logging, **urban** sprawl, and poaching, among others, thus necessitating efforts to stop such destructive behaviour rather than simply protect some species for **their perceived value.**

I think biodiversity is **a** sine qua non for **the** sustainable **functioning** of **ecosystems** because **species are** closely **interconnected,** thus **challenging** the idea that **only** certain animals **deserve protection.** For example, the **loss** of bees in **an area could cause** problems for pollination and thus **for herbivores that depend on affected plants,** eventually **disrupting** the food chain and **potentially affecting predators such as** tigers. Therefore, all animals **deserve protection,** albeit **with** differing **levels of attention** and **resources:** efforts (e.g., **sanctuaries)** to protect tigers are **tangible,** while bees **may** receive help **through** less obvious **measures to preserve their habitats.**

In conclusion, humans should endeavour to protect as many animals as possible, although **some** species may be prioritized in **terms** of **attention** and resources according to the **threats they face.**

**最值得理解的三处调整**

**1．** 原文：

> they should distinguished from other species by the threat the specific animals face and the value of the animal itself

修改后：

> they should be distinguished from other species by the threats they face and their value

情态动词后的被动结构必须是 should be distinguished。后半句用 they/their 统一指代这些动物，并用 threats 表示可能存在多种威胁，避免 specific animals、animal itself 在单复数和指代上来回切换。

**2．** 原文：

> the importance of bees is diminished by large population size and ubiquity

修改后：

> the urgency of protecting a particular bee species may be lower if it has a large and stable population

常见或数量多，并不意味着生态价值更低。修改保留原来的资源排序思路，但比较的是某种蜂类保护的“紧迫性”，而非蜜蜂的“重要性”；if 条件也避免一概认定所有蜂类都数量庞大，与下一段提到蜂类受威胁保持一致。

**3．** 原文：

> because every species is closely interconnected and is equally important, thus refuting the idea that certain animals should be prioritized

修改后：

> because species are closely interconnected, thus challenging the idea that only certain animals deserve protection

相互关联可以支持整体保护，却不能单独证明所有物种价值完全相同、资源投入必须相等。将争论对象改成“是否只有部分动物值得保护”，保留支持广泛保护的立场，同时允许结尾按风险优先分配资源，消除原文前后冲突。

## 第05篇 动植物数量下降的原因与对策 288词

One of the worrying trends in modern society is that the number of animals and plants is **declining.** This occurs for several reasons, the foremost of which is **destructive** human **activity.** Concerted efforts should be made by governments and individuals.

The underlying reason for this **trend** is human **activity. Many** forests are being threatened by deforestation for agricultural expansion, logging, and infrastructure development. A typical example can be found **in** the Amazon rainforest, **where** pristine habitats are being cleared for cattle ranching, depriving countless species of their natural homes. An additional human activity that deserves attention is the illegal wildlife trade. This practice is driven by high demand for exotic pets, traditional medicine, and animal parts, pushing many species, be they animals or plants, towards endangerment and potential extinction.

What steps can governments and individuals take to halt and reverse the trend? For governments, the keys to reversing **environmental** degradation are conservation and restoration efforts. For instance, reforestation initiatives can aim to replant native tree species in deforested areas, providing crucial habitats for various wildlife species. Individuals can also **help** by **recognizing** how their daily behaviour could lead to catastrophes for certain animals or **plants and avoiding products that contribute to this damage.** For example, a figure delicately carved in ivory **can mean** that an elephant **has been** killed for **commercial gain;** a so-called magic medicine **can involve** uncontrolled exploitation of wild plants. When the buying stops, the killing can too.

In conclusion, human activities have taken a heavy **toll** on flora and fauna; therefore, efforts, ranging from government policies to **individuals refusing to buy illegally traded wildlife products, should** be made to mitigate the problems. Otherwise, the **continued** reduction of biodiversity **could be** a recipe for disaster.

One of the growing trends in modern society is that people increasingly choose to travel abroad. This occurs for several reasons, foremost of which are **higher** per capita income and the development of the airline industry. Despite the potential adverse effects on the environment, **this trend,** on **balance,** produces positive **outcomes when its risks are managed.**

The primary reason lies in the fact that **more people** today can afford to travel internationally. As world trade thrives, the **middle** class **is expanding,** thus making it possible for **more people** to use disposable income to travel to other countries. This boom is also attributed to the fact that companies like Boeing **have produced** more efficient planes, the fact that competition **among airlines has intensified,** and the fact that budget airlines **have expanded,** all of which combined have ushered in an era of international tourism. For example, **Chinese** tourists **can take** advantage of budget airlines to travel to countries such as **Thailand.**

This boom brings cultural benefits to individuals and economic benefits to destinations. International travel exposes tourists to exotic **cultures,** ranging from what people usually eat **to** how people celebrate **local** festivals. For example, tourists can eat sushi and enjoy the fireworks during summer in Japan. In addition, international tourism contributes to greater economic prosperity. Wildlife viewing, for example, has become a source of income for African countries such as Tanzania; the Pyramids bring a large influx of tourists to Egypt, **and their spending can help raise local incomes and improve living standards.**

With this boom also **come** problems. The environmental impacts are largely attributable to the development of the hospitality industry (e.g., hotels, restaurants, and shops) and infrastructure (e.g., roads and power supply). This **can give** rise to higher levels of deforestation and construction, thus leading to damage to **wildlife habitats** and a decline in biodiversity. However, such problems **can be** mitigated by concerted efforts from governments (e.g., regulations governing sustainable development) and individuals (e.g., **environmentally responsible behaviour).** For example, **governments can restrict deforestation for tourist facilities and ban single-use** plastic **bags, while** tourists **can avoid** touching **coral reefs when** diving.

In conclusion, increased income and low-cost air travel act as **catalysts** for international tourism. It **can be** a win-win situation for tourists and tourist destinations despite the possible environmental problems, **provided that** government policies and individual **efforts keep such damage under control.**

主谓之间原来的逗号应删除；“首个杂交稻”的科学史断言在材料中没有核验。改为农业科学家的条件性例子，保留“长期研发→提高产量→改善粮食供给”的原论证功能，food supply 也比 robust food source 更准确。

**3．** 原文：

> the amount of money sports stars make is fully justifiable

修改后：

> the amount of money sports stars make is excessive when compared with the rewards received by other professionals on the basis of their social contribution

fully justifiable 支持高薪，与本篇反对立场直接冲突。改回 excessive，并明确依据是社会贡献与其他职业报酬的比较；同时正文说明稀缺技能、投入和压力也存在于其他职业，因此不能仅靠这些理由证明体育高薪合理。

# 九个话题共通的修改原则

**先对准题目中的判断。** “有作用”不等于“最重要”，“需要政府负责”不等于“只由政府负责”，“值得保护所有动物”也不等于“所有物种投入完全相同”。先明确要证明什么，再决定保留哪条因果链。

**保留长句，同时写清动作主体和结果。** 重点检查which指什么、分词动作由谁执行、并列成分是否属于同一类别。长句可以保留，但每一步要能回答“谁做了什么，因此发生什么”。

**让结论与证据的力度一致。** 改善收入不自动等于赚取利润；存在替代方案不等于任何情境都可替代；风险能够缓解也不自动证明利大于弊。必要时补上条件或比较，再在结尾重申同一立场。
