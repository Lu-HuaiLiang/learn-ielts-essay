## Water 水资源

### W01 节约用水｜292词

**题目（原稿题面）**

> The amount of drinkable water for people is decreasing. Why is this? What can be done?

采用你提供的小修版，保留问题—原因—措施结构，重点改准物品类别、污染主体与浪费造成的结果。

**小修版（加粗处为改动）**

One of the worrying trends in modern society is **the decreasing availability of** drinkable **water.** This occurs for several reasons, the foremost of which are increasing water pollution and water **wastage.** Concerted efforts from governments and individuals should be made to remedy this alarming situation.

The main cause of insufficient drinkable water is growing **water** pollution. As the population has dramatically increased over the past decades, there is an increasing need for essential items **such as** food and **clothing. The production of these items can** pollute water **sources** by releasing toxic chemicals or pesticides into the waterways, reducing the total amount of **water safe to drink.** Apart from water pollution, this problem is also being exacerbated by **wasteful** daily **habits;** for example, people may brush their teeth without turning off the tap. The sheer size of the population makes such minor **acts of** waste a serious problem, **putting further pressure on freshwater supplies.**

What steps can governments and **individuals** take to halt and reverse the trend? This requires the government to impose regulations governing industrial processes to increase the efficiency of water use and **impose** sanctions **on water polluters.** For example, **wastewater** from food and **clothing production should be treated to** meet wastewater discharge standards. Individuals can also help to deal with **this shortage** by recycling water or **avoiding** waste in their daily lives. For example, the water used for washing **vegetables** could **be reused to** water **plants;** people could turn off the tap while brushing their teeth.

In conclusion, growing water pollution and **wastage** have given rise to **a shortage of** drinkable water. Efforts should be made to change the status quo, including **enforcing regulations** and **reducing waste in daily life.** Otherwise, this disturbing trend **could be** a recipe for disaster.

**最值得理解的调整**

1. 原文：`essential items (e.g., food supply and fashion industry)`

   修订：`essential items such as food and clothing`

   items指物品，industry是行业，不能并列。后句以The production of these items作主语，明确污染来自生产过程；熟悉的因果结构得以保留。

2. 原文：`which leads to the fact some people may still have access to fresh water, but the total amount is decreasing for them.`

   修订：`putting further pressure on freshwater supplies.`

   原句没有清楚落到浪费的后果。改后形成“单次浪费少→人口众多、浪费累积→淡水供应承压”，同时保留the sheer size of the population。

3. 原文：`including imposing public policies and raising individual awareness`

   修订：`including enforcing regulations and reducing waste in daily life`

   结尾对应主体段已经展开的监管和个人节水，而不突然换成未具体论证的“提高意识”。impose sanctions on与enforce regulations也区分了处罚和执行规则。

### W02 用水限制｜341词

**题目（原稿题面）**

> some people think they have the right to water as much as they want, while some people think the government should control the use of freshwater strictly as it is limited. discuss both views and give your opinion.

题面说明：原题的the right to water as much as they want应理解为the right to use as much water as they want；water作动词通常指浇水。

采用你提供的小修版。关键是把基本用水权与无限量使用区分开，让开头、阶梯水价段和结尾保持同一立场。

**小修版（加粗处为改动）**

The importance of water **resources is beyond doubt;** however, opinions differ as to how water should be used. Some people think individuals have the right to use it freely, whereas others insist that it should be stringently controlled by governments for sustainable development. **In my view, governments should regulate excessive consumption while protecting basic water needs.**

The argument in favour of giving priority to individuals' needs is that **water is a shared natural resource. From this perspective,** no one individual or organization can lay **exclusive** claim **to it;** therefore, everyone is born with the right to use **it** freely. **Supporters therefore believe that people should be free to use** water to meet their **needs,** be it for domestic use or commercial purposes, as long as they pay for the costs involved in the treatment and transport of fresh water.

Others, however, highlight the urgent need for **governments** to impose regulations on water usage. **Despite** knowing that water is finite, individuals still waste water in their daily **lives** (e.g., leaving the kitchen without turning off the **tap). Such waste may** seem negligible; nonetheless, the sheer size of the population **means that these habits can** ultimately **contribute** to the depletion of fresh water, a problem **that could affect both current and** future generations. Governments, as the **protectors** of the **public interest,** should **adopt policies** to halt such a worrying trend.

Although individuals have **a** prima facie right to use **water, concern** for future **generations** justifies **measures** to limit **excessive** water usage. Tiered water rates, for example, are designed to **charge** lower rates **for water used to meet basic needs** and higher rates for **consumption above this level, providing an incentive to reduce excessive** use. **Therefore,** water usage **can be regulated without restricting access to water for basic needs,** while slowing down the **depletion of freshwater resources.**

In conclusion, **individuals** should enjoy the right to **meet their basic** water **needs,** while governments **should regulate excessive consumption. Tiered** water **rates offer** a way **to balance these aims** while slowing down the depletion of fresh water.

**最值得理解的调整**

1. 原文：`The argument in favour of giving priority to individuals' needs is that they have the right to do so.`

   修订：`The argument in favour of giving priority to individuals' needs is that water is a shared natural resource.`

   原文“应优先满足需要，因为他们有权这样做”接近循环论证。改后先交代支持者的资源共享观，再用From this perspective限定这是一方的理由，而非未经解释的权属事实。

2. 原文：`a problem whose adverse effects cannot be felt nowadays but are bound to plague future generations`

   修订：`a problem that could affect both current and future generations`

   去掉“现在完全感受不到、未来必定受害”两重绝对化。could表达潜在后果，既不排除当前影响，也保留关心后代的论证。

3. 原文：`individuals enjoy the right to use water as much as they want`

   修订：`individuals should enjoy the right to meet their basic water needs`

   正文支持阶梯水价，结尾就不能恢复无限用水权。按基本需求与超额用量区分单价，才能说明监管如何兼顾个人需要与资源保护。

## Technology 科技

### TE01 远程工作｜330词

**题目（原稿题面）**

> Information technology enables many people to do their work outside their workplace. Do the benefits of this mobility outweigh the disadvantages?

保留五段及would otherwise be spent的机会成本句型，修正办公成本、代词和沟通搭配，并补一句实际权衡来回答outweigh。

**小修版（加粗处为改动）**

Remote working has thrived in recent years; however, a fierce debate rages over **its** overall **effect.** Despite the disadvantages **for** interaction between colleagues, **it,** on **balance,** produces positive outcomes in the form of improved work-life balance for employees and reduced operating costs for companies.

For employees, this flexible working approach enables them to enjoy a better work-life balance. **It** enables them to save the time that would otherwise be **spent** on the routine of travelling to the office and liberates them from the increasingly intolerable commute. As they have more time to rest or spend with their family members, their sense of well-being can be greatly improved.

For employers, it **can be** cost-effective as it **reduces** the need for physical **office space.** Traditionally, companies needed to rent or buy offices to accommodate their **staff and cover** the **overhead costs associated with those premises.** However, with remote working, money that would otherwise be spent on **these** overheads could be invested in other **areas** (e.g., human resources and software) to improve **their** competitiveness, which is especially attractive for start-ups.

However, one demerit of remote working is that it poses problems **for** communication between employees. Home workers do not always have the same opportunities to connect with people as their office-based colleagues, which may take a heavy toll on group work. In addition, recruits may suffer from these working practices as they **have fewer** opportunities to communicate **face to face** or learn from other people, **reducing their access to** peer support. Nonetheless, these problems can be **partly** alleviated by holding **face-to-face** meetings regularly to forge bonds.

In conclusion, even though remote working may have an adverse effect on communication and cooperation between employees, it permits staff to enjoy more family time and companies to **improve their** competitiveness. **With such support, these daily gains in time and costs can outweigh the loss of some opportunities for spontaneous interaction.** It is part of a flexible working approach and may become the norm in the future.

**最值得理解的调整**

1. 原文：`it eliminates the need for physical bases`

   修订：`it reduces the need for physical office space`

   physical office space明确指办公场地；远程办公可能减少场地需求，不保证完全不需要。can be cost-effective也给节省成本留下条件，不把所有企业一概而论。

2. 原文：`to improve its competitiveness`

   修订：`to improve their competitiveness`

   先行词companies是复数，代词应为their。原来“省下办公室开销→投入人力与软件→提高竞争力”的长句链条完整保留。

3. 原文：`they deprive workers of the opportunities to face-to-face communicate or learn from other people, let alone peer support`

   修订：`they have fewer opportunities to communicate face to face or learn from other people, reducing their access to peer support`

   face-to-face可作前置定语，communicate后用face to face。原来的let alone不能把peer support直接并入动词结构；改为说明学习和互动机会减少如何影响同事支持。

### TE02 机器人｜283词

**题目（原稿题面）**

> Today, many tasks at home or work have been done by robots. Is this a positive or negative development?

保留效率—家务—就业—培训的原有顺序，修正机器人实例的类别，并将就业影响和最终支持立场写得有范围、有条件。

**小修版（加粗处为改动）**

Robotics has thrived for many years, revolutionizing the way people live and work; however, a fierce debate rages as to the overall effect brought about by robots. **They,** on **balance,** produce positive outcomes in the form of efficiency gains in the workplace and fewer chores to do at home despite **potential job losses in some sectors.**

At present, robotic technology is providing a helping hand for businesses, assisting humans in performing work more efficiently and sometimes more accurately. Robots, for example, **can be useful** in **large** warehouses, as they sort and move **large numbers** of objects **efficiently,** thus contributing to the **growth** of online shopping. Apart from this, **robotic technology** is also helping homeowners escape from domestic chores that they would otherwise have to do, **with robot vacuum cleaners taking over floor cleaning.**

However, **one** demerit of robotic technology **in** modern society is that it **can displace workers** in **some occupations.** With the rise of robotics and artificial intelligence, **some** people’s **jobs,** and therefore their livelihoods, **may** be **threatened by** automation. A typical example can be found in the manufacturing sector, where **workers performing repetitive tasks may become** redundant, thus making the dole queues **longer. Nonetheless,** such problems can be mitigated by helping those who are **displaced** from their jobs to focus on **upskilling or retraining,** which is **increasingly important** as **some industries become more** knowledge-intensive.

In conclusion, even though automation **can create** hardship **for affected workers,** it permits businesses to boost operational efficiency and **spares** individuals the trouble of **some** domestic chores. **On balance, I consider this a positive development, provided that** the unemployment problems that ensue from automation **are addressed** by **helping affected workers broaden their** skills **and find new employment.**

**最值得理解的调整**

1. 原文：`ranging from doing the dishes to cleaning the floor`

   修订：`with robot vacuum cleaners taking over floor cleaning`

   机器人题应举机器人实例；普通洗碗机等自动化家电不宜直接当作机器人。用扫地机器人把论据对象限定清楚，同时保留节省家务时间的功能。

2. 原文：`it is resulting in massive unemployment`

   修订：`it can displace workers in some occupations`

   某些岗位被替代不自动等于整体大规模失业。can与in some occupations限定结论范围；后面仍保留制造业重复性岗位受到影响的机制。

3. 原文：`by broadening skills to be more employable`

   修订：`by helping affected workers broaden their skills and find new employment`

   明确是谁需要拓宽技能，也说明培训的目标是重新就业。结尾改成有配套支持条件的总体赞成立场，避免一面提出新措施，一面笼统主张维持现状。

### T01 提高油价缓解拥堵｜409词

**题目（原稿题面）**

> Some people think the best way to solve traffic congestion is to increase the cost of fuel for cars. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

保留“承认油价可能有效—指出限制—提出更佳替代”的五段结构；明确收入与便利偏好的不同作用，修复成本比较，并把“必然无效”校准为条件下效果有限。

**小修版（加粗处为改动）**

A fierce debate rages as to how to mitigate congested road conditions. It is proposed that the best way to achieve this goal is to increase the price of fuel. Although this proposal can alleviate the **problem to some extent,** it **does not fully address** the **reasons** why **car use** is a **necessity** for so many **drivers,** and thus other more effective solutions **could** be **adopted.**

The argument in favour of this cost-increasing policy is that it can **help** mitigate traffic congestion. Improved per capita income **means** that a car is no longer an out-of-reach luxury for **many people, while the pursuit of convenience makes it an attractive option.** However, with this **increase in car use** comes the fact that **many** commuters are negatively **affected** by traffic, mentally and physically, especially the heavy traffic during rush hours. If **a policy of increasing** fuel **prices** were to be implemented, people might consider reducing the frequency of driving or opting for another affordable mode of transport (e.g., bus or metro), which **could** decrease the total **number of cars** on the road and, in turn, ease the increasing **congestion.**

However, **presenting** this proposal **as the best solution** is based on the **questionable** assumption that **preferences** for transport modes **depend mainly on** the cost of fuel and that **drivers have practical alternatives to private vehicles. In fact, some car owners may be less responsive to fuel prices because their preference for** private vehicles **reflects comfort and convenience, particularly where suitable public transport** is **unavailable. The** costs of **owning and using a** private **car already extend beyond fuel expenditure, so a fuel price increase alone may not be enough** to **change their travel choices.**

To mitigate traffic congestion, **two** improvements may have **more** far-reaching effects. **First,** public transport should be improved: the capacity should be increased, services extended, and routes carefully planned. With an efficient transport network, **people may voluntarily shift** from private vehicles to public **transport,** thus decreasing the number of cars on roads. The second improvement involves road construction because traffic jams still exist in small cities or villages where the number of vehicles is much **lower,** but people often get stuck in **bottlenecks that targeted road improvements could help relieve.**

In conclusion, the proposal to increase the fuel cost of cars **may have limited effects where drivers lack suitable alternatives.** Other pragmatic approaches, including **improvements** in public **transport networks** and better **road design,** could be **more effective in alleviating** traffic congestion.

**最值得理解的调整**

1. 原文：`private vehicles are much more affordable for people to travel around`

   修订：`drivers have practical alternatives to private vehicles`

   原句比较对象缺失，且与后文私家车成本较高不一致；改为检查有无可行替代方式，使反驳与开头的出行刚需衔接。

2. 原文：`shifting mode from private vehicles to public transport voluntarily takes place`

   修订：`people may voluntarily shift from private vehicles to public transport`

   以 people 作主语，让“自愿”明确修饰人的选择；may 保留服务改善可能促成换乘的论证，不把行为变化写成必然。

3. 原文：`is ineffective in view of the flawed assumption`

   修订：`may have limited effects where drivers lack suitable alternatives`

   “成本以外还有因素”不能推出涨价完全无效；结论限定适用条件，并与前文承认其可能减流量保持一致。

### T02 无车日的利弊｜366词

**题目（原稿题面）**

> Some people think there should be a Vehicle-free day, which means private cars, trucks and motorbikes are banned in the city center. And only the bus, bicycles and taxis are permitted in the city center. Do you think the benefits outweigh the disadvantages? (to what extent do you agree or disagree?)

保留支持无车日的立场与五段顺序，按题目明确允许的交通方式；修正发动机与车辆、体验与接受、服务吸引力等概念，并补足公共服务投入与持续收益的实际权衡。

**小修版（加粗处为改动）**

One of the worrying trends in modern society is that the proliferation of cars in urban areas is **contributing** to growing traffic congestion and **harmful** air pollution. One possible solution is a vehicle-free day when only **buses, bicycles and taxis** are **permitted** in the city centre. **On** balance, **its potential to** ease traffic and reduce pollution **outweighs** the **additional demands on** public **services, provided that adequate transport capacity is available.**

The argument in favour of **a vehicle-free** day is that it can help reduce air pollution and relieve traffic problems by decreasing the number of vehicles. During rush hours, the daily parade of **motor vehicles** clogs the city centre streets and **their exhaust fumes pollute** the air, plaguing commuters and exposing children to **pollution that may pose health risks.** If this policy is adopted, then there **may** be fewer vehicles on the roads, which can directly address the issues to some extent.

Moreover, this policy **can act** as a catalyst for **greater** public awareness. During this day, **more** people **may** travel by public transport; by doing so, passengers **may come** to **recognise its potential** advantages **through direct experience: cost savings, time savings** and **lower emissions.** Gradually, **this experience** can have a far-reaching effect on the problems **if** it reduces their dependence on private cars and fosters public awareness about green travel.

However, not everyone is convinced by such benefits. **Critics** argue that during the vehicle-free day, the flow of passengers **may** dramatically increase. If public transport services are not improved accordingly, then the advantages mentioned above **may be** diminished because **passengers may no longer regard public transport as an attractive option,** thus in turn **encouraging greater reliance on private vehicles after** the **event.** However, this problem can be strategically addressed **as part of** the **policy:** increasing budget allocation to provide **adequate** services to the public. **This additional expenditure can also improve services beyond the event itself, helping to justify its cost.**

In conclusion, although it may require better public services, the **potential benefits of easing** traffic and **mitigating** pollution **outweigh the costs of improving services, particularly when the additional capacity also benefits everyday passengers. A vehicle-free day could therefore become** a **regular event** in the future.

**最值得理解的调整**

   修订：`exposing children to pollution that may pose health risks`

   原文直接断言污染导致肺部感染，具体因果缺少依据；保留污染风险这一论证功能，用 may 和较宽泛的健康风险避免把医学因果写成已证实。

2. 原文：`passengers are prompted unconsciously to accept the advantages of public transport: money-saving, time-saving and eco-friendly`

   修订：`passengers may come to recognise its potential advantages through direct experience: cost savings, time savings and lower emissions`

   unconsciously 与“通过体验认识优点”不符；改清体验过程，并将冒号后的项目改为并列名词，避免把省钱省时效果写成无条件保证。

3. 原文：`the urgent need to ease traffic and mitigate pollution necessitates a vehicle-free day, which should be a norm in the future`

   修订：`the potential benefits of easing traffic and mitigating pollution outweigh the costs of improving services, particularly when the additional capacity also benefits everyday passengers. A vehicle-free day could therefore become a regular event in the future`

   原结尾只强调需求迫切，尚未回答利弊权衡；补明增运力可持续惠及日常乘客，使为何值得投入与正文呼应，并降低“必须实施”的绝对力度。

### T03 全天免费公共交通｜384词

**题目（原稿题面）**

> Some people think the best way to solve traffic congestion in cities is to provide free public transport 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

保留否定“最佳方案”的立场，分别评价免费与全天服务；修复交通成本的比较、虚拟条件和主谓搭配，并将必然失败改为相对于针对性改善的成本效益判断。

**小修版（加粗处为改动）**

There has been a fierce and unresolved debate as to how to tackle the problem of traffic congestion in urban areas. Some **people** propose that 24/7 free public transport can be the most successful **approach.** Even though this proposal **could help** tackle the **problem,** in view of its efficacy and feasibility, it is far from the best solution.

The effort to offer free public transport **may have a limited effect on** traffic **problems if services remain inconvenient. Presenting it as the best solution** presumes that people’s travel **preferences are** strongly **influenced** by commuting **costs and that** free public transport **will attract substantial numbers** of **car users.** However, **some** car owners **may give relatively little weight to fares,** as **they already accept** the **higher** costs of **owning and using a car in exchange for comfort and convenience. Unless** public transport **can offer comparable convenience, removing fares alone may not persuade these drivers** to **switch.**

In addition, the proposal to offer 24/7 services **may be difficult to justify in areas with low overnight demand.** It assumes **that** the **current transport system’s inability** to **meet late-night travel** demand could be **addressed** by providing 24/7 services, making public transit more **attractive** and thus reducing the number of cars on roads. However, **extending services overnight may do little** to **relieve peak-hour congestion,** as traffic congestion often occurs during rush hours (e.g., between 8 am **and** 9 am). Moreover, if this policy were to be adopted, **operating** costs **could rise** due to the extra pay for working unsocial hours, which **could place additional pressure on** the government budget.

To alleviate traffic jams in cities, two improvements may have **more** far-reaching effects. First, public transport should be improved: the capacity should be expanded, services **extended where demand is high,** and routes carefully planned. With an efficient transport network, **people may voluntarily shift** from private vehicles to public **transport,** thus decreasing the number of cars on roads. The second improvement involves **targeted** road construction, which is easy to understand because people often get stuck in bottlenecks, and therefore **improvements at these locations** can relieve congestion.

In conclusion, **although** the 24/7 free policy **could benefit some passengers, its limited appeal to some drivers** and **potentially high costs mean that it may** be **less cost-effective than targeted improvements to public transport and road bottlenecks.**

**最值得理解的调整**

**小修版（加粗处为改动）**

The contribution made by general education to individuals and **societies is clear.** However, with increasing occupational differentiation comes the belief that **universities should concentrate on** one particular subject **so that students can specialize.** Even though some people advocate specialization, there **are good reasons why** a wide range of subjects **should be offered** at universities.

The argument in favour of specialization is the **focused use** of **effort** and attention, which facilitates achieving more desirable results. Rather than merely **studying** a wide range of subjects, students could concentrate more **fully** on one academic speciality, which **can help** them deliver better academic performance. This **can contribute** to scientific and technological advancement because specialists **working at** the **frontiers** of knowledge **may be better equipped to make breakthroughs; this** can also **help students climb** the **career** ladder because industrial society is increasingly knowledge-intensive. It **may seem** sensible, therefore, for **a** university to **concentrate its teaching on** one subject.

However, the **advantages of specialization do not mean** that **universities should exclude other subjects, since** general learning **can complement** professional competence. In effect, understanding the basic **principles** in **a range of** fields **helps** students understand better what they already know, and **equips them** to sharpen their skills and knowledge within one particular subject. In terms of professional competence, **well-rounded** job candidates are truly educated human beings who can perform their special tasks better precisely because they have general **education** as well as **intensive** specialized training. For example, an industrial designer and **businessman who takes** a calligraphy **class may be able to use this broader learning to improve product design.**

In conclusion, it is necessary to **retain** general education at university, while specialization should also be valued, both of which **can help individuals** to succeed in the future.
